# Founder guide

Live: [aibusiness.fun/guide](https://aibusiness.fun/guide)

This is the handbook for getting real work out of Aura OS. Token, LP, and VPS runbooks stay in the other files under `docs/`.

## First hour

1. **Wake the company** — one honest sentence: what you sell, where, who pays. Skip token pages until the desk has a job. [`/access`](https://aibusiness.fun/access)
2. **Write the strategy once** — Settings → Strategy is injected into every agent. City, offer, voice, what you will not do.
3. **Give one mission this week** — one number, one deadline. “12 bookings from Instagram this month, €0 ads.”
4. **Connect a channel or mailbox** — drafts without a channel stay in the app. Outreach without a mailbox never sends.
5. **Approve the first three** — leave Autopublish off until three drafts look like you.
6. **Share the week** — Week in review → Share this week. Quiet weeks stay zeros. That is the product working.

## Talk like a boss

Atlas reads company name, city, strategy, and knowledge on every turn.

## Honest rules

- Workforce **Active** means a real queued or running task.
- The worker ticks about every **ten minutes**. Not a fake busy meter.
- Mailbox outreach is draft-then-you-send. **No silent email.**
- You can run the whole OS **without a token or an NFT**. Official AURA CA only on [aibusiness.fun](https://aibusiness.fun) and X `@bihary41418` — never a DM.
- Nachbar guest points ≠ company AURA ≠ founder progress.
- Google review links are optional and unpaid. Confirm in-store is the mint gate for guest stamps.
- **KYC (Didit)** is required for the token sale and live trading. The $29 OS seat does not need it. Start it on `/identity`. We store status only.
